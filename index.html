OUR STORY

8+ Years Of Experience We Provide Food Services

With over 8+ years of experience in serving delicious and authentic food, Pohewala has grown from a simple idea into a trusted name for quality meals. What started as a passion for bringing the true taste of poha to people has now become a commitment to serve fresh, hygienic, and flavorful food every day. Pohewala was founded by Mr.Chahul and Mr.Pawan, two passionate food lovers with a vision to make traditional flavors easily accessible to everyone. Their journey began with a shared belief that simple food, when made with the right ingredients and care, can create unforgettable experiences. We believe food is not just about taste—it’s about comfort, tradition, and connection. That’s why every dish at Pohewala is prepared with carefully selected ingredients, traditional recipes, and a touch of love. Over the years, we have proudly served thousands of happy customers, constantly improving our menu and service to meet their expectations. Whether it’s a quick breakfast, a fulfilling meal, or bulk orders for events, we are here to deliver quality you can trust. At Pohewala, our journey continues with one simple goal—to serve happiness in every bite.
